# Fact check of site/index.html, November 2026

Checked on 2026-11-10 against the file `site/index.html` as it was read that day. Scope: route stops, dated items for 2026-27, glossary, whole-system diagram and transfer list, FAQ, profile and compare explainers, data labels, footer.

Method: official pages were fetched or searched (Rijksoverheid, VO-raad, Nuffic, KiesMBO, Studiekeuze123, OCO Amsterdam, Belastingdienst). Where a statement rests on a search summary and not on a page I opened in full, it is marked "(search summary)". The corrected texts contain no em or en dashes.

## Summary

| Verdict | Count |
|---|---|
| wrong | 1 |
| outdated | 1 |
| imprecise | 10 |
| ok but hedge | 6 |

Most important, in order:

1. Havo diploma to vwo is a legal right with no conditions since 1 Aug 2020 (issue 1). The site says "the school decides".
2. Primary registration: the site omits the "at least 10 weeks before the fourth birthday" rule and says "no national deadline"; some cities (Amsterdam) have their own deadlines by birth date (issue 3).
3. FAQ "Do I need an international school?" calls international schools "mostly private" and mentions "international departments in primary school". Dutch International Schools are government funded, and the bilingual primary route is tpo (issues 4 and 5).
4. The 2026-27 dates on the page are correct. Add the placement date (from 1 April 2027) and the latest admission decision date (12 May 2027) (issue 12).
5. hbo propedeuse to wo: the official text says a propedeuse gives admission to wo, subject to programme requirements. The site understates this (issue 8).

### 5. "International departments in primary school" (imprecise)

### 6. Lottery FAQ (ok but hedge)

Location (quote): "Where a school has more applicants than places, a lottery can be used. It is not how most of the country works, as far as we know. Verdict: ok but hedge. "As far as we know" is honest, but lotteries are used in several of the largest regions. Also, the Rijksoverheid says schools may not use extra tests to refuse a child, except schools that test for specific skills (for example bilingual or top-sport schools). "Some schools also select, for example on a test or an essay" is therefore too broad.

Corrected text: "Where a school has more applicants than places, a lottery can be used. Procedures are regional and change from year to year. Amsterdam runs a central lottery and matching procedure. In the Utrecht regions a lottery is held only at schools with more first-choice applications than places. In Haaglanden, eight schools drew lots in 2026. Rotterdam and Eindhoven also have their own rules. A secondary school may not refuse a child because of test scores. The exception is a school that tests for specific skills, such as a bilingual (tto) or top-sport school. Check your region and ask your child's school."

Sources:

## Verified as correct

- Provisional advice 10 to 31 Jan 2027; doorstroomtoets 25 Jan to 12 Feb 2027; final advice by 24 Mar 2027; sign-up week 25 to 31 Mar 2027.
- Child may start primary school at 4, may register from 3, settling-in days from 3 years 10 months for up to 5 days, compulsory attendance from the first day of the month after the fifth birthday.
- Test advice higher than provisional advice leads to an upward adjustment unless the school explains why not. A secondary school may not require a single-level advice and must place at least at the advised level.
- A secondary school may be refused only on limited grounds; you may register at more than one school.
- School decides on repeating a year; no legal rules; school must inform you; objection through the complaints procedure; the old age rule for group 3 ended in 1985.
- vmbo takes 4 years with 4 learning pathways; pathway and profile choice by the end of year 2; havo 5 years, vwo 6 years; four profiles in havo and vwo.
- mbo registration by 1 April keeps the right to admission; later registration gives no right.
- mbo level 4 gives the right to hbo; havo gives access to hbo; vwo gives access to wo; havo does not give direct access to wo.
- 21+ test minimum age 21.
- Newcomer provision: a temporary newcomer provision is capped at two years of enrolment; secondary ISK from age 12.
- Special primary schools need a formal decision (toelaatbaarheidsverklaring).
- Public schools are not based on a religion; bijzondere schools are.
- Tto network has more than 130 secondary schools; tto exams are the normal Dutch exams.
- Glossary entries for groep, schooladvies, doorstroomtoets, vmbo/havo/vwo durations, mbo levels 1 to 4, hbo, wo, brugklas, sbo, IB, Montessori and Steiner.
