You will hear these words (glossary)
groep
year group (1 to 8 in primary school)
schooladvies
school advice, the level your child's school recommends
doorstroomtoets
the end-of-primary test
vmbo, havo, vwo
the three main secondary levels, 4, 5 and 6 years
mbo
vocational college, levels 1 to 4
hbo
university of applied sciences
wo
research university
brugklas
the first year of secondary school
ISK
newcomer class for Dutch learners aged 12 and up
tto
bilingual secondary education: a large part of the lessons is taught in English
IB
International Baccalaureate, an international programme and diploma
technasium
a secondary school stream where pupils work on real design and research assignments for outside clients
Montessori, Dalton, Jenaplan, vrije school
school concepts with their own teaching method. A vrije school follows Steiner (Waldorf) education
sbo
special primary school
